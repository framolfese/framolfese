## 🎯 About Me

I'm a Ph.D. student in AI and Natural Language Processing at **Sapienza University of Rome**, working with the [SapienzaNLP](http://sapienzanlp.uniroma1.it) research group. Recently, I was an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AGI (May 2025 - October 2025).

### 🔬 Research Interests

My research focuses on:
- 🧠 **Large Language Models** - Understanding and improving LLM capabilities
- 🤔 **Reasoning** - Developing systems that can reason effectively
- ✅ **Evaluation** - Creating better methodologies for assessing LLMs and AI systems
- ❓ **Question Answering** - Advancing QA systems and their reliability
